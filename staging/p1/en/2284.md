A bank booked about $606 million in fees and revenue on a set of Malaysian bond deals, and for a while that number looked like pure profit. The second number arrived years later, in a courtroom in Brooklyn, with a guilty plea attached. It was more than four times larger.

[[story]]
Tom Wright and Bradley Hope's *Billion Dollar Whale* is about the 1MDB affair, and the court record has kept growing since the book's pages; this story follows what it added about the bankers and the bank. On 1 November 2018 the US Attorney's Office in Brooklyn announced that Tim Leissner, a former Goldman Sachs partner in Asia, had pleaded guilty to conspiracy to launder money and to violate the Foreign Corrupt Practices Act. He agreed to forfeit $43.7 million.

Prosecutors charged two others the same day. Roger Ng, another former Goldman banker, had been arrested in Malaysia at the request of US authorities. Jho Low, the financier the DOJ said had worked as an intermediary on the deals, was indicted and was still at large when the settlement came two years later.

The bank came next. On 22 October 2020 The Goldman Sachs Group entered a deferred prosecution agreement, and its Malaysian subsidiary pleaded guilty in federal court in Brooklyn to conspiring to violate the anti-bribery provisions of the FCPA. Goldman agreed to pay more than $2.9 billion in a coordinated resolution with authorities in the United States, Britain, Singapore, Malaysia and elsewhere. The FBI described it as the largest penalty ever paid to US authorities in an FCPA case.

The department listed what had counted against the bank. Goldman had not voluntarily disclosed the conduct. The bribes totalled more than $1.6 billion and went to at least 11 foreign officials. Goldman had also delayed handing over recorded phone calls in which its staff discussed allegations of bribery. For that reason it received only partial credit for cooperation, and its penalty was cut by 10 percent from the bottom of the guideline range.

The individuals took longer. In April 2022 a federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Ng on all counts, and in March 2023 Judge Margo Brodie sentenced him to ten years in prison; he denies the charges. In May 2025 she sentenced Leissner to two years, according to court reports.

Set the first number beside the later ones. The bank earned about $606 million in fees and revenue. The bill ran past $2.9 billion, and by 2025 two of the bankers had been sentenced to prison.

[[lessons]]
$$**Weigh a shortcut by the bill that comes later, not by the first payment.** The early reward is easy to count, since it arrives as a fee or a bonus or a closed sale. The cost is slow, scattered and unpleasant to estimate, so it tends to be left out of the sum. Picture a landlord who skips an inspection to fill a flat quickly: the first month's rent is certain, while the repair, the dispute and the lost tenant arrive across the following year. Before you take a shortcut, write both columns, and fill the second one first, since the first will always be easy to write.$$
