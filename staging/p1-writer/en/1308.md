[[story]]
Lift a heavy bar and the fibres in a muscle tear at a microscopic level. The ache that usually follows comes with the body repairing them, and it rebuilds them stronger than before. No tear, no growth. Nassim Nicholas Taleb's word for what comes next is overcompensation: the body rebuilds a little more than the damage required, as if preparing for the stress to return.

Taleb uses this in *Antifragile* as his picture of something antifragile: it needs some stress, and it gains from it. Take the stress away and the damage never happens, so the strengthening never follows. A muscle that is never asked to lift anything has nothing to rebuild.

##The body is built to grow from the very thing that damages it.##

The immune system works in a similar way. In Taleb's telling, a child who meets ordinary germs early builds up defences, while a child raised in a sterile setting may end up with a more fragile immune system. Exposure is how it learns.

Learning follows the same pattern. A problem you first get wrong and then work out tends to stick, while an answer handed over on a plate is forgotten quickly. Taleb extends the idea to firms that learn from small failures and to people who grow through hard periods.

He adds one condition: the dose matters. The strain has to be one the system can recover from, because stress beyond that point injures instead of strengthening. A weight that is too light does little and one that is too heavy hurts, so the work lies in finding the load that can be lifted and recovered from. The bar has to go back on the rack.

[[lessons]]
$$**Choose stress you can recover from.** A person who dreads speaking in public may avoid every chance and stay nervous for years. A better choice is to give one short talk to a few colleagues, rest, and look at what went well and what did not. That lets her stretch a little at a time and keep the strain at a level she can recover from, so she has a chance to come back from each attempt a little more capable than before. If a talk goes badly, the rest between attempts is part of the plan and not a sign she should stop.$$
