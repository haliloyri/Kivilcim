Microphones set above the ground at a waterhole picked up no sound at all. The elephants who came to drink still reacted to an alarm call that nobody could hear in the air. The researchers had set up the silence on purpose.

[[story]]
In *An Immense World*, Ed Yong describes how every animal lives in the world its own senses allow. For elephants, one of those channels runs through the ground. They may feel vibrations in the earth through receptors in their feet and trunks. How that works is still debated, and one proposal is that vibrations travel from the toenails through bone to the ear. Their low rumbles travel through the ground as well as the air, so a message carried through the earth is plausible.

In Etosha, in Namibia, a research team set out to test it with a recording made in 1994 of the alarm calls of a herd that was confronted by lions. To separate ground from air, the team needed a call that existed only in the soil.

A device turned that recording into vibrations in the soil. Microphones above the ground confirmed that no sound could be heard in the air. The elephants had no way to hear the call with their ears. The setup pulled ground and air apart.

The elephants that visited the waterhole reacted to the vibrations as they had reacted to the audible call.

##There was no sound in the air, and the elephants got the alarm anyway.##

They were selective, too. They responded more to vibrations recorded nearby than to those from other areas. Vibrations recorded in Kenya and played in Namibia were ignored.

The experiments suggest elephants could pick up seismic signals made by other elephants from as far as about two kilometres. That is an estimate rather than a measurement, and the mechanism is not settled. Still, the notion of a herd communicating where the microphones hear nothing is surprising on its own.

[[lessons]]
$$**If you did not hear a signal, check which channel it came through.** Say you text a friend, get no reply, and decide they are not interested. They may have come to your door that same day to ask; the message channel stayed silent while another one was open. Before you decide something is absent, ask how the other person is actually speaking, and you avoid reaching the wrong conclusion and learn what the silence actually meant.$$
